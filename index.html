How often should I use these packs?
We recommend using our face packs 2 to 3 times a week. For sensitive skin, start with once a week.
Are these suitable for sensitive skin?
Absolutely. Our products contain only pure herbs. However, we always recommend a patch test.
How to store the face pack powder?
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the lid tightly closed to prevent moisture.
